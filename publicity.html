The problem

Customers waste time guessing which places are truly halal — mixed menus, unclear sourcing, weak trust.

Our idea

One customer app for verified partners only: browse real kitchens, order fast, follow delivery until it arrives.

Why join now

You’re early in Porto. Get first access, shape what we list, and help set the EU standard for halal delivery.

How customers use EatHalal

  1. Discover Open EatHalal and browse verified partners near you — restaurants, grills, and more.
  2. Choose with trust See clear halal signals and real food photos before you add to cart.
  3. Order & receive Checkout in a few taps, then follow status until the bag is at your door in Porto.
