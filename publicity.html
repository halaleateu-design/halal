The problem

Customers waste time guessing which places are truly halal — mixed menus, unclear sourcing, no trust signal.

Our idea

One app that only lists verified partners. Clear “Muslim-Made” food, nearby restaurants on the map, and live delivery status.

Why join

You’re early in Porto. Shape the menu, get first access, and help build the EU’s halal delivery standard.

How customers use EatHalal

  1. Discover Open the app — see recommendations and restaurants near you on the map.
  2. Choose with trust Open a dish with halal badges, spice level, and real kitchen photos.
  3. Order & track Checkout, then follow the rider live until the bag is at your door.
